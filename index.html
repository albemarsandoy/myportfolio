About Me

Who I am and how I work.

I graduated with a BS in Accountancy from Western Mindanao State University in 2026, and I'm currently reviewing for the CPA Licensure Examination. I'm also a Civil Service Professional eligible, having passed the paper-and-pencil exam.

Before and during my review, I worked as a virtual assistant supporting several US-based companies — handling CRM systems, client communication, marketing campaigns, and day-to-day operations. I've also taught accounting as a Teaching Assistant Intern, designing lessons and handling grading for around 150 students across three sections.

I like work that rewards attention to detail — whether that's reconciling numbers or keeping a client's pipeline organized. I'm adaptable, deadline-driven, and comfortable picking up new tools quickly.
